# The Office Uprising

A small, original 3D office adventure with chunky, colorful late-1990s console RPG proportions. No packages or internet connection required.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. Move with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**, press **E** near a coworker to speak with them, and press **Space** to rally during the final confrontation. Touch controls appear on mobile.

Recruit coworkers by talking to them. Recruited coworkers will speak to others nearby every 15–20 seconds, creating a domino effect. Coworker dialogue stays open until you press **E** again; recruitment updates appear separately. Once six of eight have joined, approach CEO Tonald Drump at the north end of the office and rally the team. Reduce Tonald’s HP to zero to trigger the team’s jumping and confetti celebration. The movement seeks livable wages, real time off, and decent treatment.

All art is software-drawn in canvas. Characters, company, and events are fictional and deliberately absurd.
